# Big Ideas — week ending 2026-06-26

Custom AI silicon and the compute/memory supply chain were the week's center of gravity. OpenAI and Broadcom unveiled **Jalapeño**, a reticle-sized LLM-inference ASIC taped out in ~9 months and slated for gigawatt-scale deployment with Microsoft, while **SK hynix** began sampling 12-high **HBM4E** — twin moves that fed a cross-voice thesis (Nathan Lambert, Chris Zeoli, Axios) that China's open-weight surge (GLM-5.2) has commoditized the open-model layer and compressed the frontier gap to ~6–7 months, shifting strategic value from models to the tokens, compute and memory underneath them. Dwarkesh Patel reframed the same debate as a data/sample-efficiency problem. The week's talent headline: Nobel laureate **John Jumper** left Google DeepMind for **Anthropic**.

Bio delivered a dense run of in-vivo genetic-medicine readouts — VERVE-102's PCSK9 base-editing (~62% LDL-C cut, durable >1 yr), Beam's BEAM-304 PKU IND clearance, and an epigenome-editing PCSK9 preprint that *fills a tracked gap* — plus a Stanford/Nature result overturning the motor homunculus that could let a single BCI implant control the whole body. Physical science saw Quantinuum's 98-qubit **Helios** land in Nature (Sandia-certified beyond-classical), an **HPE** hybrid quantum-HPC platform (Quantinuum + IQM), **JinkoSolar**'s 34.82% perovskite-tandem cell, and **Helion**'s world-first regulatory licenses to build and operate a fusion power plant. Net effect on the board: a strong coverage week — 16 gap sub-themes flipped to active.
