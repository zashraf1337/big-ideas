# Big Ideas — week ending 2026-07-10

A dense, gap-filling week that pushed the board's coverage sharply upward. On the AI side the story was less about a single frontier model and more about **interfaces and reality checks**: OpenAI shipped **GPT-Live**, a full-duplex voice model that listens and speaks at once (delegating reasoning to GPT-5.5 in the background), while ICML 2026 and ACL 2026 handed Outstanding-Paper honors to work probing the failure modes of RL — deception probes that catch reward hacking ("The Obfuscation Atlas") and a fix for entropy collapse in RLVR. Against that, a Berkeley/industry benchmark ("Agents' Last Exam", 1,000+ real tasks across 55 industries) found frontier agents averaging just **2.6%** on its hardest tier despite Terminal-Bench scores up to 82% — a sober counterpoint to agent hype, and a thesis echoed by an independent trend of agents starting to do real economic work (Remote Labor Index at 15.8%; a near-autonomous AI chemist running 10,080 wet-lab reactions). The week's loudest debate was infrastructure: Meta's plan to resell "excess" AI compute triggered a semiconductor selloff even as record grid demand argued for scarcity, not glut.

The physical sciences and bio did the heavy lifting on **filling long-dormant gaps**. Energy alone activated three: ESS launched a sodium-ion Bridge BESS (with ~$1B in demand signals via Alsym cells), Illinois approved ComEd's first virtual power plant, and Plug/Orica reached FID on Australia's largest green-hydrogen hub. EarthDaily's constellation crossed its commercial-ops satellite threshold; Realta Fusion demonstrated first-of-kind direct plasma-to-electricity conversion on its WHAM mirror machine; and Sydney/IBM pinned mid-circuit measurement as a dominant QEC error source (logical survival <90%→>96%). Bio was carried by verified conference/regulatory backfill: the FDA expanded **CASGEVY to children as young as 2**, Lilly's oral **orforglipron beat oral semaglutide head-to-head** (ACHIEVE-3), and the allogeneic/in-vivo cell-engineering and CAR-T-for-solid-tumors gaps both activated (Caribou CB-011; AstraZeneca/Neogene NT-175). Net effect: **+10 active sub-themes**, gaps down from 24 to 14, and BIO, QUANTUM and ROBOTICS now at 100% coverage.
