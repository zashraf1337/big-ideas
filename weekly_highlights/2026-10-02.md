# Big Ideas: week ending 2026-10-02

OpenAI's DevDay on Sep 29 led the week. GPT-6.1 Sol lands near GPT-6 Astra quality at about one-fifth of Astra's token price. OpenAI also introduced Dots, always-on background agents inside ChatGPT, and the Agents API now runs computer use and multi-agent work on OpenAI's own hosted infrastructure. Simon Willison, CNBC and Gizmodo all read the event as OpenAI becoming an agent-and-distribution platform rather than a model vendor, and Altman ruled out a 2026 IPO, tying the timing to safety confidence. The second trend is a turn toward formal accountability: Reuters reports the FTC is investigating OpenAI and Anthropic over consumer risks, and Cal Newport (added as a new voice) called for a Congressional inquiry into the labs. A context-management paper co-authored by Nathan Lambert and Luke Zettlemoyer (Context Language Models) argues models should manage their own context, which matters for long-horizon agents.

EASD in Milan was the week's biggest bio event. Full TRIUMPH-2 data for retatrutide showed 20.8% weight loss at 80 weeks in people with T2D (Lancet). Lilly's eloraTZP, an amylin agonist combined with tirzepatide, reached 23.3% in Phase 2b, and Boehringer's survodutide trailed at 13.1% (NEJM). On the physical side, Starship Flight 14 finally reached orbit and deployed 26 Starlink V3 satellites on a shortened mission. In quantum, Infleqtion reported 30 error-detected logical qubits just as Microsoft and Qolab (Martinis) proposed a stricter four-condition "scalable logical qubit" bar. Also this week: CFS placed a record HTS-tape order with Fujikura, Cerebras signed 100 MW of inference capacity with Gimlet Labs, Google flew TPUs to orbit for Project Suncatcher, and the NRC issued the first US utility SMR construction permit.

Codex gained cloud environments and an /agents delegation UI. A proposed MCP Events spec lets plugins trigger on events. [src](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/10/openai-devday-2026/)

Dots are always-on, Astra-powered assistants that take assigned tasks, connect to apps and keep working in the background. They launched alongside ChatGPT Space, a shared workspace for teams and agents. [src](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/29/openai-dev-day-2026-dots-space-sol)
- **IROS 2026 award finalist: VTAP visuo-tactile active-palm gripper** (humanoid_robotics) _[backfill]_ _[IROS 2026]_: Purdue and Columbia's VTAP gripper is listed in the IROS 2026 Best Paper / Best Student Paper session. It pairs tactile fingertip arrays with an actuated visuo-tactile palm and handles 3 mm object singulation, in-hand syringe reorientation and peg-in-hole insertion. [src](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15448)
- **Lilly eloraTZP (amylin + tirzepatide) hits 23.3% in Ph2b; Ph3 starts Q4** (glp1) _[EASD 2026]_: At EASD on 30 Sep 2026, eloralintide plus tirzepatide gave 23.3% weight loss at 48 weeks in 367 adults with T2D and obesity, versus 14.8% for tirzepatide alone. A1C fell up to 2.9 points, and Phase 3 starts in Q4 2026. [src](https://www.biospace.com/drug-development/superior-weight-loss-blood-sugar-control-propel-lillys-amylin-gip-glp-1-combo-into-phase-3)
- **Survodutide SYNCHRONIZE-2: up to 13.1% weight loss in T2D (NEJM)** (glp1) _[EASD 2026]_: Boehringer presented the Phase 3 trial at EASD on 1 Oct 2026, with simultaneous NEJM publication. In 755 adults with T2D, the glucagon/GLP-1 dual agonist cut weight by up to 13.1% at 76 weeks (placebo 3.1%). That trails triple agonists such as retatrutide. [src](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/01/3372564/0/en/boehringer-ingelheim-s-survodutide-achieves-up-to-13-1-weight-loss-in-new-phase-iii-trial-alongside-significant-improvements-in-glycemic-control-in-people-with-obesity-and-type-2-d.html)
- **Infleqtion entangles 30 error-detected logical qubits from 80 neutral atoms** (quantum_error_correction) _[backfill]_ _[Quantum World Congress 2026]_: Announced 24 Sep at Quantum World Congress. Infleqtion used 10 blocks of the distance-2 [[8,3,2]] code on its Sqale system with post-selection, which is error detection rather than full correction. Infleqtion says its 100-logical-qubit target for 2028 is on track. [src](https://infleqtion.com/infleqtion-achieves-30-entangled-logical-qubits-on-its-sqale-quantum-computer/)
- **Microsoft/Qolab propose four-condition test for a 'scalable logical qubit'** (quantum_error_correction) _[backfill]_: Troyer, Nayak and Martinis set four conditions: repeated QEC, universal fault-tolerant gates with real-time decoding, logical error falling with code size, and a path to large qubit counts. Error-detected 'logical qubit' counts would fail the bar. [src](https://quantum.microsoft.com/en-us/insights/blogs/scalable-logical-qubits)
- **Google launches 4 TPUs to orbit on Project Suncatcher prototype satellite** (ai_accelerators): Google's Planet-built Suncatcher prototype was set to launch on SpaceX Transporter-18 from Vandenberg on 1 Oct 2026, carrying four TPUs and about 1 kW of solar power. It is a technology-validation mission rather than an operational orbital data center. A two-satellite optical-link test is planned for 2027. [src](https://convergedigest.com/google-project-suncatcher-orbital-ai-data-center-tpu/)
- **Cerebras to supply Gimlet Labs with 100 MW of CS-4 inference capacity** (ai_accelerators): Announced 30 Sep. Cerebras will deploy CS-4 wafer-scale systems for Gimlet's cloud over one to two years, with service starting in 2027. General Compute signed a multi-year Cerebras deal a day earlier. [src](https://qz.com/cerebras-gimlet-labs-ai-chips-cloud-inference-092826)
- **CFS places largest-ever HTS tape order (10,000+ km) with Fujikura for ARC** (fusion): Commonwealth Fusion Systems ordered more than 10,000 km of high-temperature-superconducting tape from Fujikura for ARC magnets, starting with the Virginia plant. CFS is ordering ahead of SPARC reaching Q>1. [src](https://cfs.energy/news-and-media/commonwealth-fusion-systems-places-largest-single-/)
- **Context Language Models: the model edits its own context as a file** (ai_dev_ecosystem): Shao et al. (with Nathan Lambert, Zettlemoyer and Koh) propose models that rewrite their own context as a file. They report +11.4% accuracy at 21.5% fewer FLOPs on BrowseComp-Plus and large gains on a 24-hour multi-repo agent-swarm task. [src](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37725)
