# Big Ideas — week ending 2026-10-16

Model news led the week. Meta Superintelligence Labs shipped Muse Wonder, its first frontier-class model ($4/$20 per M tokens, with a self-reported "active memory" edge). Google opened Gemini 4 Argon to developers at an introductory $2/$10 that doubles in December, and Microsoft used OCP Global Summit to unveil Maia 300 as its primary in-house inference chip from 2027. The most telling engineering disclosure came from Anthropic: merged changes went from ~2,500/day in May to 30,000+/day in September, so it replaced Git with an in-house system (Scion) plus AI-assisted review (Antique).

Elsewhere it was mostly schedule news. Rocket Lab slipped Neutron to 2027, Blue Origin is targeting December for New Glenn's return, and Musk said Starship Flight 15 (~late Nov) will try the first ship tower catch. Bio was quiet in primary sources; the main item is a reported ~$200M FNIH/NCI therapeutic cancer-vaccine initiative ahead of Moderna/Merck's full Ph3 data at ESMO on Oct 24. On the voices side, Karpathy's "capability funnel" post was the week's most-discussed framing. Quantum had no verifiable in-window results.
