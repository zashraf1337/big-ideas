## New category candidates

**One new Tier-1 candidate (correcting 09-04).**

### Agent-to-instrument interfaces & self-driving labs — domain AI (cross-links BIO, HARDWARE/ROBOTICS)

**Why:** On 2026-08-27 Anthropic opened a research preview of the **Model Hardware Standard (MHS)** — a shared specification/driver layer so AI agents can operate physical lab and factory devices (microscopes, liquid handlers, robot arms, plate readers) through uniform read/write commands, with device safety limits and physical parameters carried in the driver. Launch partners include Genentech (BCA protein assay automated across liquid handler + arm + plate reader), a UW Baker/Pinglay-lab PhD student (agent-supervised qPCR, arm↔liquid-handler handoffs), Carnegie Mellon and AWS. Anthropic says it intends to publish MHS openly after the preview. Primary: <https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-hardware-standard-research-preview>. Coverage: <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/27/anthropic-pushes-into-physical-world-with-new-standard-to-help-ai-agents-operate-machines.html> · <https://qz.com/anthropic-model-hardware-standard-ai-robots-lab-equipment-082826>

The field already names the broader area as its own category — **self-driving laboratories (SDLs)**: Nature Reviews Chemistry review (July 2026) framing the next phase around scalability, generalizability and provenance-complete experimentation <https://www.nature.com/articles/s41570-026-00847-2>; Communications Materials on multi-agent orchestration of autonomous materials labs <https://www.nature.com/articles/s43246-026-01219-5>; Nature news on autonomous robot labs in biology <https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-00453-8>; RSC Digital Discovery Korea SDL survey <https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2026/dd/d6dd00024j>; arXiv on measurement-access risk in autonomous scientific control <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.05696>.

**Taxonomy fit:** PARTIAL at best. Closest existing sub-themes are `ai__ai_for_science__autonomous_research_hypothesis_generation` (hypothesis generation — the *thinking* half, not the *hands*) and `ai__agents_developer_ecosystem__agent_primitives_mcp_skills_sdks` (software protocols, not hardware). Nothing on the board covers physical actuation of instruments by agents or closed-loop experimentation infrastructure. MHS is to lab hardware what MCP was to software tools, and MCP got its own sub-theme.

**Recommend: YES — add to TAX in `scripts/build_taxonomy.py`.** Suggested: theme `ai__ai_for_science`, sub-theme `self_driving_labs_agent_instrument_interfaces`, track BOTH, example "Anthropic Model Hardware Standard (research preview)", source_url the Anthropic post. Seed items: MHS, the Nature Reviews Chemistry review, the Communications Materials paper.

**Also note:** this exact item was flagged as "noise, no resolving URL" on 09-04. The aggregator was right and the verification filter was too strict. Worth remembering that a search-summary claim with a plausible primary source deserves one direct fetch of the vendor's news page before being dropped.
