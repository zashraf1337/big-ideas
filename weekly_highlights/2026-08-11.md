# Big Ideas — week ending 2026-08-11

Heavy week on the agent/AI front, and a deliberate catch-up on a category the board had been missing. OpenAI's internal **Astra** model produced Lean-4-verified solutions to ten long-open math/CS problems for ~$2,000 of inference compute; Alibaba shipped **Qwen3.8-Max** (2.4T-param MoE, 1M context) at roughly a quarter of GPT-5.6's price with open weights promised within a week; **Meta** entered the coding-agent race with Muse Code + Muse Spark 1.2; and **Microsoft's Agent Framework Harness** reached GA as a governance layer over third-party agents. Prompted by a direct question from Zubair, we closed a real blind spot: **spec-driven / "prompt-less" development** (GitHub Spec Kit, Amazon Kiro, Tessl) is now a named 2026 movement, added as a curated sub-theme with watch-list players — which took the AI domain to 100% sub-theme coverage.

Bio delivered the week's biggest regulatory milestone: the FDA approved **Moderna's mFLUSIVA**, the first mRNA seasonal-flu vaccine. It was offset by setbacks — **CagriSema** missed HbA1c non-inferiority vs tirzepatide, a second delayed child-death disclosure hit China's investigator-led **CRISPR/DMD** trials, and Jennifer Doudna's **Aurora Therapeutics** scrapped its lead PKU program. On the physical side, **CFS** crossed $4B raised (first pension/SWF money into fusion), **ITER** passed two-thirds of its tokamak core, **Blue Origin** traced the New Glenn explosion to a BE-4 oxygen valve, and the **Hot Chips 2026** program confirms Google's 8th-gen TPU and OpenAI's first in-house-silicon talk. The cross-cutting story: frontier players are racing to own their silicon (Anthropic, Tesla/SpaceX's $16.8B Terafab, AMD's Taalas buy), even as AI "slop" starts outpacing the benchmarks and CVE databases meant to catch it.
