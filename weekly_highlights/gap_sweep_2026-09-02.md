### 1. REFUTED — personalized mRNA cancer vaccines are *not* short of 2026 evidence

The last sweep's most consequential open question was that `bio__rna_nucleic_acid_therapeutics__personalized_mrna_cancer_vaccines` had **no fresh 2026 evidence** in the whole scan. That is wrong, and the miss was large.

On **2026-08-19**, twelve days before that sweep, Merck and Moderna announced **positive topline Phase 3 INTerpath-001** results: intismeran autogene plus Keytruda met its primary endpoint (recurrence-free survival) and its key secondary endpoint (distant metastasis-free survival) at a pre-specified interim analysis in 1,137 patients with completely resected Stage IIB–IV melanoma. This is the **first Phase 3 win for an individualized neoantigen therapy** — a category-defining readout for a sub-theme the last sweep described as evidentially empty.
https://www.merck.com/news/merck-and-moderna-announce-phase-3-interpath-001-trial-of-intismeran-autogene-plus-keytruda-met-endpoints-of-recurrence-free-survival-rfs-and-distant-metastasis-free-survival-dmfs-in-patient/

The same sub-theme also has a **fresh negative** datapoint, inside this sweep's window: on **2026-08-28** BioNTech terminated the Phase 2 BNT122-01 trial of autogene cevumeran as adjuvant monotherapy in resected Stage II/III colorectal cancer after the DSMB flagged a numerical OS imbalance (no new safety signal); the pancreatic combination trial continues.
https://www.biontech.com/int/en/home/mediaroom/news/statements/2026/08/BioNTech-Provides-Update-on-Phase-2-Clinical-Trial-of-Autogene-Cevumeran-in-Resected-Colorectal-Cancer.html

So the correct 2026 framing is bimodal — a landmark Phase 3 success and a Phase 2 termination in the same fortnight — not "no evidence."
