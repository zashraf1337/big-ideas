[src](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27374)

[src](https://www.embl.org/news/science-technology/alphafold-database-adds-viral-protein-complexes-to-support-pandemic-preparedness/)

[src](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/24/3368730/0/en/prime-medicine-announces-u-s-fda-clearance-of-investigational-new-drug-application-for-pm647-in-alpha-1-antitrypsin-deficiency.html)
- **IonQ runs real-time QEC decoder for 408 logical qubits on a laptop CPU** (quantum_error_correction) _[IEEE QCE 2026 follow-on / IonQ research disclosure]_ — On Sep 22, 2026 IonQ demonstrated the industry's first end-to-end real-time quantum error decoder running on a single commodity CPU (Apple M4 Max), handling simulated circuits with up to 408 logical qubits and 31.5M quantum operations with only ~0.02% added processing overhead. [src](https://www.ionq.com/news/ionq-demonstrates-industrys-first-end-to-end-real-time-quantum-error-decoder)
- **Soochow University/LONGi nano-scaffold pushes perovskite-silicon tandem to 34.0%** (renewables_batteries) _[backfill]_ _[Science Bulletin]_ — Published Sep 10, 2026 in Science Bulletin, a ZrO2 nano-scaffold interfacial design from Soochow University and LONGi's Central R&D Institute lifted a perovskite-silicon tandem cell to 34.0% lab efficiency (33.5% certified steady-state) with a record 2.014V open-circuit voltage, retaining 84% efficiency after 2,000 hours. [src](http://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/09/10/longi-soochow-university-unveil-34-0-perovskite-silicon-tandem-solar-cell-based-on-dual-anchored-interfacial-design/)
