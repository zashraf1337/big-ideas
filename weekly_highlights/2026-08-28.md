# Big Ideas — week ending 2026-08-28

Hot Chips 2026 (Aug 23–25) was the week. Nvidia gave the first public architectural detail on Rubin and Vera, AMD detailed MI455X at 432GB of HBM4 and the 2.9-exaflop Helios rack, and OpenAI walked on stage with Jalapeno — its first custom inference ASIC, built with Broadcom, designed in nine months, claimed at roughly 50% better cost-efficiency than merchant GPUs with volume production in 2027. The venue also closed two of the board's three long-standing hardware gaps in a single afternoon: Opticore's homodyne photonic tensor crossbar gave us a real, primary-sourced item under photonic/optical compute, and UC Berkeley's Gemmelos dual-chip edge SoC (alongside KU Leuven's ETHEREAL and Harvard/Lockheed's Pistil) finally put something under consumer/edge NPUs. Board coverage moved from 126 active / 3 gaps to 128 active / 1 gap.

Bio had a genuinely heavy week too. FDA approved tirzepatide for cardiovascular risk reduction in type 2 diabetes on Aug 28 — the first GIP/GLP-1 dual agonist to carry a CV label — and Moderna/Merck posted the first positive Phase 3 for an individualized neoantigen mRNA cancer vaccine. Charité Berlin published a six-patient CD19 CAR-T reversal of refractory rheumatoid arthritis in Nature Medicine, three of them in drug-free remission at up to a year. On the commentary side, one thesis showed up everywhere independently: the binding constraint on frontier AI has moved past chip supply to power delivery, skilled trade labor, and capex financing. Dylan Patel argued it on Dwarkesh, Ben Thompson framed Apple's and OpenAI's hardware moves as two bets on the same problem, and OpenAI itself pitched Jalapeno on perf-per-watt rather than peak FLOPs.

## New this week

**AI accelerators / advanced compute**
- Nvidia detailed the Rubin GPU and Vera CPU at Hot Chips, claiming Vera Rubin NVL72 delivers 10–30× more tokens per megawatt than GB300 NVL72 on SemiAnalysis' AgentX benchmark at high interactivity. [[Hot Chips deck](https://hc2026.hotchips.org/assets/program/conference/day1/FINAL_NV_HC2026_Rubin.pdf)] [[Nvidia](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/vera-rubin/)]
- AMD disclosed MI455X — 432GB HBM4 at 23.3TB/s, 40.26 PFLOPS MXFP4 — with the 72-GPU Helios rack at 2.9 exaflops, 31TB HBM4 and 1.7PB/s bandwidth, shipping Q3 2026. [[Hot Chips deck](https://hc2026.hotchips.org/assets/program/conference/day1/FINAL_AMD%20Instinct%20MI400%20GPU%20Architecture_HotChips.pdf)]
- OpenAI unveiled **Jalapeno**, a Broadcom-built inference ASIC scaling to a 2,048-chip domain over Tomahawk6 switches, ~50% better cost-efficiency than merchant GPUs, volume production 2027. [[OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-broadcom-jalapeno-inference-chip/)]
- **Opticore** showed a large-scale homodyne photonic crossbar for AI tensor processing, claiming up to 100× the energy efficiency and 25× the compute density of leading GPUs on standard foundry processes. **Fills the photonic-compute gap.** [[poster](https://hc2026.hotchips.org/assets/program/posters/Opticore_Hotchipsposter.pdf)]
- UC Berkeley's **Gemmelos**, a two-chip Intel 16nm platform for multimodal edge inference. **Fills the consumer-NPU gap.** [[poster](https://hc2026.hotchips.org/assets/program/posters/UC%20Berkeley%20Gemmelos%20Poster%20HotChips26.pdf)]

**GLP-1 agonists** — FDA approved Mounjaro (tirzepatide) on Aug 28 to reduce MACE in adults with T2D at high CV risk, on SURPASS-CVOT (13,299 patients, HR 0.92 vs dulaglutide). First GIP/GLP-1 dual agonist with a CV label. [[Lilly](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/fda-approves-lillys-mounjaro-tirzepatide-to-reduce-cardiovascular-risk-in-adults-with-type-2-diabetes-302862415.html)]

**mRNA vaccines** — Moderna/Merck's intismeran autogene + pembrolizumab met both RFS and DMFS endpoints in Phase 3 INTerpath-001 in resected stage IIB–IV melanoma. First positive Phase 3 for a personalized neoantigen mRNA therapy. [[Moderna](https://news.modernatx.com/merck-and-moderna-announce-phase-3-interpath-001-trial-of-intismeran-plus-keytruda-met-endpoints-of-rfs-and-dmfs-in-melanoma)]

**CRISPR therapies** — Charité Berlin's phase 1 COMPARE trial: 6 RA patients refractory to up to 8 prior therapies, all with markedly reduced disease activity, 3 in sustained drug-free remission at up to a year. [[Nature Medicine](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04603-3)] Separately, the **smACGmax** editor achieved simultaneous triple-base (A/C/G) editing at 5.7–41% efficiency with up to 3.2× more allelic diversity than dual A-C editors. [[CRISPR Medicine News](https://crisprmedicinenews.com/news/triple-base-editor-diversifies-three-nucleotides-at-once/)]

**BCI** — FDA cleared Paradromics' Connexus to connect via Convey to patients' own laptops, tablets and phones in the Connect-One study without per-device sign-off. [[Paradromics](https://paradromics.com/news/paradromics-receives-fda-approval-to-expand-bci-access-to-personal-computing-devices-in-connect-one-clinical-study/)]

**Protein design** — The **Human Bindome** released 306,146 designed binder candidates against 8,296 human proteins (41% of the proteome) from an accelerated BindCraft pipeline, each with predicted structure and confidence. [[bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.30.741542v1)]

**Transformers / LLMs** — Z.ai confirmed "Ox Alpha" is **GLM-5.3-Flash**: 320B params / 18B active, natively multimodal MoE, 1M context, MIT license, ~1/10th the price of GLM-5.2. [[TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/08/27/zhipu-identifies-ox-alpha-as-glm-5-3-flash-and-releases-model-weights/)]

**Self-driving** — Nevada Transportation Authority unanimously approved robotaxi permits: Tesla 5,000 vehicles, Waymo 1,000, Uber 1,000 over 12 months in Clark County. Tesla testified it expects ~2,500 deployed by next year. [[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/tesla-uber-and-waymo-all-get-the-ok-to-operate-thousands-of-robotaxis-in-nevada/)]

**Humanoid robotics** — Tiangong Ultra ran the 100m in 8.86s (semifinal, Aug 26) then 8.64s (final) at the World Humanoid Robot Games, up from its own 9.39s on Aug 22. [[CGTN](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2026-08-26/Chinese-humanoid-robot-sprints-100m-in-8-86-seconds-breaks-own-record-1PV0aSpZ91S/p.html)]

**Reasoning models** — Inherent's 27B Qwen3.6-based agent **Faraday** reportedly outperformed Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 at independently reproducing published findings without being given answers. [[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/22/inherent-founded-by-deepmind-alumni-says-its-ai-teammate-just-outperformed-anthropic-and-openai-at-replicating-research/)] Also: Anthropic/EPFL showed self-propagating "mind virus" payloads can jump between agents via editable persistent-identity prompt files, with a one-paragraph warning cutting spread to near zero. **Fusion** — Inertia Enterprises and LLNL cut cryogenic D-T fuel-layer production from ~a week (NIF) to 2–3 hours, targeting sub-hour with Inertia's 10 MJ laser. [[ANS](https://www.ans.org/news/2026-08-25/article-8335/inertia-and-llnl-accelerate-fusion-fuel-manufacturing/)]

**Reusable rockets** — Ship 41 completed a 60-second six-engine static fire Aug 21, its last qualification milestone before Flight 14 (internally NET Sept 15, first orbital payload deployments planned). [[NSF](https://www.nasaspaceflight.com/2026/08/ship-41-engine-testing-flight-14/)]

**Renewables + batteries** — HZB hit 30.3% on a co-evaporated perovskite-silicon tandem using an ultra-thin CsCl seed layer in a solvent-free process. [[TechXplore](https://techxplore.com/news/2026-08-cesium-chloride-seed-layer-vacuum.html)]

**Quantum error correction** — D-Wave published a Swap-Wait-Swap CZ gate between dual-rail cavity qubits at ~99.9% two-qubit fidelity, ~500ns, ~0.5% erasure rate, post-selected residual Pauli errors below 0.1%. [[Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10822-y)]
