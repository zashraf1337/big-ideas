## New category candidates

Areas the field is now explicitly naming as their own category that fit **no** existing sub-theme. Recommendation column = whether to add to `TAX` in `scripts/build_taxonomy.py`.

### BIO — strongest signal this sweep (whole modalities the taxonomy doesn't name)

BIO shows 100% coverage, but only across the modalities it lists. The field in mid-2026 is loudly organizing around several modality/platform categories with no home in our taxonomy.

- **Radiopharmaceuticals / radioligand therapy (RPT)** — *Recommend: YES.* IQVIA names it a top-growth R&D modality by deal volume; therapeutic segment ~13% CAGR; broad pharma M&A. A whole modality (not small molecule, biologic, or cell/gene therapy) with no slot. https://www.iqvia.com/blogs/2026/04/radiopharmaceuticals-take-center-stage-in-global-rd · https://blog.crownbio.com/radiopharmaceuticals-the-evolving-role-of-radiation-in-targeted-cancer-therapy
- **Antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) & next-gen conjugates** — *Recommend: YES.* IQVIA calls ADCs a "pillar of oncology innovation"; 2,334 conjugates tracked (Jan 2026, +88% YoY in novel formats); dominant at AACR/ASCO 2026. https://www.iqvia.com/blogs/2026/04/antibody-drug-conjugates-a-pillar-of-oncology-innovation · https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40164-026-00804-1
- **Targeted protein degradation (PROTACs & molecular glues)** — *Recommend: YES.* First-ever PROTAC FDA approval (vepdegestrant, May 2026) validated the modality; >20 molecular-glue degraders in trials; opens "undruggable" target space. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12937832/ · https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0169409X26001560
- **Xenotransplantation** — *Recommend: MAYBE.* FDA cleared first in-human pig-organ trials (2025–26); gene-edited source pigs; ~70+ patients slated. Distinct field (germline-scale editing + transplant medicine) unmatched by our somatic-editing sub-themes; slightly narrower/slower than the three above. https://www.kidneyfund.org/article/fda-greenlights-first-clinical-trials-genetically-modified-pig-kidney-transplants-humans · https://nyulangone.org/news/first-gene-edited-pig-kidney-transplant-clinical-trial-begins-nyu-langone-health
- **Organoids / organ-on-chip & new approach methodologies (NAMs)** — *Recommend: MAYBE.* FDA CDER draft guidance (Mar 2025) + FDA/NIH $150M push to make animal testing "the exception" is reshaping preclinical dev; a named, regulator-driven category. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-025-02690-0 · https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/biotech/fda-and-nih-pledge-more-flexibility-and-new-150m-investment-animal-testing-alternatives
- **Spatial omics / spatial multi-omics** — *Recommend: MAYBE.* Distinct discovery-platform category (spatial transcriptomics/proteomics + dedicated foundation models); sustained methods surge per Nature Methods. Our "AI x Bio" theme covers models generically but not the spatial measurement modality. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02997-5 · https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/mco2.70713

### ENERGY

- **Thermal energy storage / industrial heat batteries** — *Recommend: YES.* A dedicated Thermal Battery Alliance (Antora, Rondo, Electrified Thermal, RedoxBlox) targets industrial heat (~12% of US GHG); >750–1800°C systems can serve ~75% of US industrial heat demand; Rondo turned on its first large heat battery at an oil field. Distinct from our LDES sub-themes, which cover grid *electricity* storage, not high-temp heat. https://www.energy-storage.news/thermal-energy-storage-tech-analysis-rondo-antora-malta-inc-mga-1414-energynest-polar-night-and-brenmiller/ · https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/energy-storage/innovation-antora-massive-heat-battery
- **AI-driven electricity demand & behind-the-meter clean-firm power** — *Recommend: MAYBE.* Repeatedly named the biggest grid story of 2026; hyperscalers bypass the public grid with behind-the-meter generation and 20-yr clean-firm PPAs. Cross-cuts several supply-side sub-themes; the *demand-and-procurement* category itself is explicitly named but overlaps existing energy + hardware slots — decide whether to add or route into existing homes. https://www.morganstanley.com/insights/articles/powering-ai-energy-market-outlook-2026 · https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-technology-perspectives-2026/executive-summary

### HARDWARE

- **Analog / in-memory computing (compute-in-memory)** — *Recommend: YES.* A distinct named compute paradigm, separate from both Neuromorphic and Photonic sub-themes. IBM analog-AI inference chips (NorthPole, ~14x better perf/watt), new analog in-memory *training* algorithms (IBM/ETH Zürich), ReRAM/PCM crossbars. Targets the energy/memory wall with a non-von-Neumann, non-photonic approach. https://research.ibm.com/blog/analog-in-memory-training-algorithms · https://semiengineering.com/llms-on-analog-in-memory-computing-based-hardware-ibm-research-eth-zurich/

Big because power procurement is now the binding constraint. Caveat: mostly 2028–2030+ energization → strategic-watch, not near-term. Overlaps the ENERGY "AI-driven demand" candidate above — pick one home. https://introl.com/blog/nuclear-power-ai-data-centers-microsoft-google-amazon-2025 · https://www.iaea.org/bulletin/data-centres-artificial-intelligence-and-cryptocurrencies-eye-advanced-nuclear-to-meet-growing-power-needs
